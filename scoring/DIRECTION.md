# Direction

Work within the paper's problem space: step-level scheduling for colocated
prefill and decode LLM inference under TTFT and TPOT SLOs.

Valid improvements include better FairBatching variants, more accurate
time-budget calibration, improved admission control, and additional baselines.
Do not switch to unrelated paradigms such as changing the model, changing the
trace, or optimizing a different latency objective.
